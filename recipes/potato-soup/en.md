## Preparation

1. Place a saucepan over medium heat and cook the bacon until it achieves a crisp texture. Rely solely on the rendered bacon fat for cooking, omitting any additional oil.
2. While the bacon cooks, chop the spring onions into thin rings.
3. Take the crisp bacon out of the pan and set it aside, then toss the white portions of the spring onions into the leftover bacon fat and sauté briefly.
4. Peel your potatoes and chop them into medium-sized, uneven chunks.
5. Transfer the potato chunks into the saucepan along with the milk, bringing the mixture to a gentle simmer for roughly 10 minutes.
6. Once the potatoes are tender, blend the mixture until smooth using an immersion blender. Ladle the soup into two serving bowls and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper as needed.
7. Crumble the reserved bacon and top each bowl of soup with the crunchy bits, a handful of grated mozzarella, and the fresh green spring onion rings.
8. Serve immediately and savor your meal!
