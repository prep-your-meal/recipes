title: "Sauerkraut & Smoked Pork Skillet with Schupfnudeln"
---

## Preparation

1. Start by removing the skin from the onion and chopping it finely.
2. Core and seed the bell pepper, then slice it into manageable, bite-sized chunks.
3. Dice the smoked Kasseler pork chop into similar bite-sized pieces.
4. Warm half of the rapeseed oil in a skillet over high heat. Toss in the pork cubes and sear them for roughly 5 minutes, making sure to stir from time to time.
5. Toss the chopped onions and bell peppers into the skillet with the meat, continuing to sauté for another 2 minutes.
6. Stir in the tomato paste, mixing thoroughly until well incorporated.
7. Incorporate the sauerkraut into the pan, combine all the ingredients, season to taste with salt and pepper, and pour in the water. Put a lid on the skillet and let the mixture simmer over medium heat for about 8 minutes.
8. Meanwhile, warm the remaining rapeseed oil in a separate pan. Add the Schupfnudeln and fry them for approximately 5 minutes, stirring periodically until golden.
9. Mince the fresh parsley finely.
10. Combine the cooked Schupfnudeln directly into the sauerkraut pan and toss well. Plate the dish, and finish each portion with a dollop of sour cream and a sprinkle of fresh parsley before serving.
