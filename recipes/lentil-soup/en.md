---

## Preparation

1. Pour the vegetable broth into a spacious pot, bring it to a rolling boil, and stir in the lentils. Reduce the heat to medium and allow them to cook gently for roughly 45 minutes.
2. While the lentils are cooking, thoroughly rinse the carrots and leeks, then cut them into neat rounds and rings, respectively.
3. Finely dice the onions. Transfer the onions, carrots, and leeks to a skillet and sauté them briefly over dry heat without using any oil.
4. Transfer the cooked vegetables into the pot with the lentils. Pour in additional vegetable broth if the mixture seems too thick, and let the soup simmer for an extra 15 minutes.
5. Taste the dish, adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper as needed, and ladle into bowls to serve.
