## Preparation

1. Begin by combining the rice and water in a spacious pot, setting it over medium heat until it reaches a gentle simmer.
2. While the rice heats up, core and deseed the bell peppers, then chop them into manageable, bite-sized chunks.
3. Proceed to drain the canned corn and kidney beans thoroughly.
4. Incorporate the chopped peppers, drained legumes, and diced tomatoes directly into the pot with the rice, stirring to combine all the ingredients evenly.
5. Mince or press the peeled garlic cloves right into the mixture. Season with your chosen spices, reduce the heat, and let the dish simmer for approximately 25 minutes, making sure to stir from time to time.
6. Serve and enjoy!
