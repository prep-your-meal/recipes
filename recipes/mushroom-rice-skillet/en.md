## Preparation

1. Wipe the mushrooms clean and cut them into thin slices.
2. Remove the skins from the onion and garlic; mince the onion finely and crush or press the garlic.
3. Heat half of the olive oil in a large skillet, add half of the mushroom slices, and sauté for 5 minutes before removing them to a separate plate.
4. Using the same pan, cook the onion and garlic in the remaining olive oil for roughly 2 minutes, then toss in the remaining mushrooms and cook for an additional 5 minutes.
5. Mix the brown rice into the pan, pour in the vegetable stock, cover with a lid, and allow it to gently simmer for approximately 20 minutes.
6. While the rice cooks, chop the scallions into thin rounds.
7. After the cooking time is complete, gently stir the freshly sliced scallions and the previously set-aside mushrooms into the rice, then add salt and pepper to suit your palate.
8. Plate your dish and dig in!
