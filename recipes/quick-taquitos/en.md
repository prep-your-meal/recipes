## Preparation

1. Set your oven's temperature to 180 °C (350 °F) so it heats up properly.
2. Pour the liquid out of the canned chickpeas. Transfer the legumes to a mixing bowl and use a fork, along with a splash of water, to mash them into a smooth, thick paste.
3. Prepare the avocado by cutting it in half, removing the pit, peeling away the skin, and slicing the flesh into small, bite-sized cubes.
4. Arrange the tortillas on a clean flat surface. Spread the mashed chickpeas evenly across each one, followed by the avocado cubes, most of the cheddar cheese, and a generous amount of fresh coriander—be sure to reserve a small portion of both the cheese and coriander to use as a finishing touch later.
5. Sprinkle salt and pepper uniformly over the fillings.
6. Form the taquitos by rolling the tortillas up firmly. Transfer them into a baking dish, ensuring they are placed with the seam side facing downward to hold their shape, and scatter the reserved cheddar over the top.
7. Place the dish on the center rack of the oven and bake for approximately 15 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
8. Take the baking dish out of the oven and scatter the remaining fresh coriander over the finished taquitos before serving.
