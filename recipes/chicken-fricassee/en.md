## Preparation

1. Prepare the long-grain rice following the directions on the package.
2. Remove the skins from the onions and roughly chop them.
3. Roughly chop one-third of the total carrots. Place them in a saucepan along with the chopped onions and vegetable broth, then bring the liquid to a boil.
4. Season the chicken breast fillets with salt and pepper, place them into the boiling broth mixture, and let them gently simmer on low heat for a quarter of an hour, making sure to turn the meat over halfway through the cooking time.
5. While the chicken simmers, cut the rest of the carrots into tiny dice and open, empty, and rinse the canned peas.
6. Take the cooked chicken out of the pan. Using an immersion blender, puree the remaining broth and vegetables until completely smooth, then incorporate the milk.
7. Toss the diced carrots and drained peas into the velvety sauce and return the mixture to a boil.
8. Slice the chicken breasts into small, bite-sized portions, return them to the pot, and let everything simmer together for 3 minutes.
9. In a small cup, dissolve the cornstarch in a splash of cold water, then pour this slurry into the sauce to achieve a thick consistency.
10. Plate the finished chicken fricassee while hot, accompanied by the freshly prepared rice.
