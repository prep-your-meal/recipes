---

## Preparation

1. Begin by slicing the chicken breasts into small, bite-sized portions.
2. Heat a non-stick skillet and sear the chicken pieces dry—without any oil—for roughly 5 minutes until lightly browned.
3. While the chicken cooks, peel and finely mince the garlic clove, and cut the carrots into quarters.
4. Sprinkle the chicken with curry powder, salt, and pepper, then toss in the prepared garlic and carrots, cooking everything together for about 3 minutes.
5. Pour in the water to deglaze the pan, add the whole grain pasta directly to the mixture, and allow it to simmer for approximately 8 minutes.
6. Pour in the milk and cream, stirring well, and let the dish simmer for an additional 2 minutes.
7. As it finishes, dice the mozzarella into small cubes and stir them gently into the pasta until completely melted and creamy.
8. Serve immediately and enjoy!
