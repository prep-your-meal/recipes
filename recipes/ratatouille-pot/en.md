## Preparation

1. Start by removing the skins from the onion and garlic, then mince the onion finely.
2. Remove the core from the bell pepper, and chop it into bite-sized chunks along with the zucchini and eggplant. Slice the cherry tomatoes in half.
3. Warm the olive oil in a spacious pot over medium warmth, and cook the chopped onion for roughly 2 minutes until translucent.
4. Toss in the cubed eggplant, press the garlic directly into the vessel, and continue cooking the mixture for an additional 3 minutes.
5. Pour in the vegetable broth to release any browned bits, then incorporate the zucchini, bell pepper, and tomatoes. Combine thoroughly, sprinkle in the paprika, salt, pepper, and oregano, and allow the mixture to gently bubble for about 10 minutes, giving it an occasional stir.
6. Stir in the tomato paste and balsamic vinegar to finish, cook for 5 more minutes, and plate immediately.
