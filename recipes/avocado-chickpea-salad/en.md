title: "Avocado Chickpea Salad"
---

## Preparation

1. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, peel away the skin, and chop the flesh into small, bite-sized cubes.
2. Open the can of chickpeas, drain the liquid thoroughly, and transfer the legumes into a mixing bowl alongside the prepared avocado.
3. Remove the outer skin of the onion, mince it finely, and cut the feta cheese into small cubes. Incorporate both ingredients into the bowl.
4. Mince the fresh cilantro leaves finely, mix them into the salad, and finish by seasoning with salt and pepper according to your preference.
5. Serve and enjoy your creation!
