## Preparation

1. Prepare the pasta following the package guidelines until al dente.
2. Strain the cooked pasta well and move it into a spacious mixing bowl.
3. Warm one-third of the total olive oil in a skillet over a medium flame. Season the chicken breast with salt and pepper, then sear in the pan, turning occasionally, until nicely browned and thoroughly cooked.
4. Take the chicken out of the skillet, cut it into convenient, bite-sized portions, and mix it into the waiting pasta.
5. Slice the romaine lettuce into pieces and cut the cherry tomatoes in half. Toss both directly into the bowl with the pasta and chicken.
6. Prepare the dressing by extracting the fresh lemon juice into a compact bowl. Incorporate the yogurt, mustard, garlic powder, and the leftover olive oil, whisking everything until smooth and cohesive.
7. Pour half of the prepared dressing and all of the parmesan cheese into the salad mixture, tossing gently to coat everything evenly. Plate the salad across individual dishes and finish by drizzling the remaining dressing over the top.
8. Serve immediately and enjoy your meal!
