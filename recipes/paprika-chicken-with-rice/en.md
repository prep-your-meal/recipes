---

## Preparation

1. Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil.
2. Stir in the rice, sprinkle with salt, reduce the heat, and allow it to simmer gently for 15 minutes.
3. Incorporate the lentils directly into the pot and continue cooking the mixture for an additional 15 minutes until tender.
4. While the grains are cooking, slice the chicken breast and bell peppers into delicate, uniform strips, then season well with curry powder, sweet paprika, black pepper, and salt.
5. Heat coconut oil in a non-stick skillet and sauté the seasoned chicken and peppers until cooked through and vibrant, then plate alongside the warm lentil and rice blend.
