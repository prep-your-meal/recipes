---

## Preparation

1. Begin by wiping the mushrooms clean and rinsing the broccoli thoroughly. Slice the mushrooms and break the broccoli down into bite-sized florets.
2. Mince or press the garlic cloves and place them directly into your skillet.
3. Dice the chicken breast into manageable chunks. Transfer the chicken to the skillet with the garlic, drizzle in half of the sesame oil, and cook until golden brown and crispy.
4. While the chicken cooks, cut the spring onions on a diagonal. Toss the sliced onions, mushrooms, and broccoli into the pan with the chicken, and let everything sauté together for roughly 7 to 10 minutes.
5. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the water, soy sauce, honey, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Pour this liquid mixture into the skillet to deglaze the pan, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.
6. Serve hot and savor your dish!
