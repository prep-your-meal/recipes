## Preparation

1. Start by bringing a pot of water to a rolling boil, then peel the sweet potato.
2. Chop 50% of the sweet potato into large pieces and allow them to boil gently for roughly 10 minutes until tender.
3. While the first batch cooks, cut the remaining sweet potato and the carrot into small dice, and strain the chickpeas.
4. Remove the skins from the onion and garlic, and finely mince the onion.
5. Warm the olive oil in a saucepan, add the minced onion, and cook for approximately 2 minutes until it becomes soft and translucent.
6. Mince the garlic directly into the pan, incorporate the ground cumin along with the grated ginger, and continue cooking for an additional 2 minutes to release their aromas.
7. Toss in the raw sweet potato cubes, carrot pieces, and drained chickpeas, letting everything sauté together for 4 minutes.
8. As the vegetables cook, use a fork to thoroughly mash the boiled sweet potato chunks until smooth.
9. Pour in the coconut milk to lift any browned bits from the bottom of the pan, then thoroughly blend in the mashed sweet potato and the apple-mango puree.
10. Scatter the frozen peas into the mixture and incorporate the flour to help thicken the base.
11. Mince the fresh parsley finely and stir it into the pot along with pinches of salt and black pepper. Allow the mixture to gently bubble over low heat for about 10 minutes.
12. Serve and enjoy your dish!
