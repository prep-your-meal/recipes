---

## Preparation

1. Begin by thoroughly rinsing the bell peppers. Carefully cut off the stem ends and use a knife to clear out all the interior seeds and membranes.
2. Dice both the onion and the tomato into small, even pieces.
3. Place a non-stick skillet over heat and cook the ground beef alongside the chopped onion until the meat is fully browned, skipping the need for extra oil.
4. Sprinkle the beef mixture with chili powder, garlic powder, cumin, salt, and black pepper, adjusting the amounts to suit your palate.
5. Stir the diced tomatoes, black beans, corn, and salsa directly into the skillet, blending everything well before allowing the contents to come to a gentle, short simmer.
6. Spoon the hearty beef and vegetable filling generously into each hollowed bell pepper, sprinkle shredded parmesan cheese over the top, and place them in the oven to bake at 180°C (350°F) for roughly 30 minutes.
7. Serve and savor your meal!
