title: "Cauliflower Rice with Avocado Cream"
---

## Preparation

1. Trim away the core and outer leaves of the cauliflower, separate it into individual florets, and process them in a food processor or blender until the texture mimics grains of rice.
2. Cut the spring onions into thin rounds and finely mince the parsley.
3. Place the cauliflower rice into a mixing bowl, then combine it with the prepared spring onions and chopped parsley.
4. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, scoop out the flesh, and transfer it to the food processor. Pour in the lime juice and season with salt, turmeric, and fresh basil leaves, processing until the mixture achieves a smooth, velvety consistency.
5. Pour the blended avocado dressing over the cauliflower rice mixture, stir well to ensure everything is evenly coated, and plate immediately.
