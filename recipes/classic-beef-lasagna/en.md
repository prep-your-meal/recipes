## Preparation

1. Begin by removing the skins from the onion and garlic, and mince the onion finely.
2. Warm some olive oil in a suitable pot, introduce the chopped onion, and cook it gently for roughly 2 minutes.
3. Mince or press the garlic directly into the pot, continuing to cook for an additional minute.
4. Incorporate the ground beef, breaking it apart with a utensil, and cook until it is thoroughly browned and crumbly—this should take about 5 minutes.
5. Pour in the diced tomatoes and vegetable broth to deglaze the pan. Season with salt, pepper, and oregano, and allow the mixture to simmer uncovered for approximately 10 minutes.
6. Set your oven to preheat to 180°C (350°F).
7. For the white sauce, melt the butter in a separate small saucepan, then whisk in the flour to form a cohesive roux.
8. Slowly stream in the milk while whisking vigorously to prevent lumps. Season the mixture with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg until smooth.
9. Cook the béchamel over gentle heat for about 3 minutes, making sure to stir constantly until thickened.
10. Lightly coat the bottom of a baking dish (roughly 25.5 x 16.5 cm) with a portion of the meat sauce, then arrange a single layer of lasagna noodles over it.
11. Continue building the dish by adding more meat sauce, a drizzle of the béchamel sauce, and a generous third of the combined mozzarella and parmesan cheeses.
12. Alternate the layers of pasta, meats, white sauce, and cheeses until all components are finished, ensuring the final layer is topped entirely with cheese.
13. Place the dish on the middle rack of the preheated oven and bake for about 30 minutes, or until the topping is beautifully golden and bubbling.
