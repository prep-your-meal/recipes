## Preparation

1. Warm the olive oil in a skillet set over medium heat. Add the ground beef and cook for roughly 5 minutes, breaking it apart with a utensil until thoroughly browned and crumbly; season with salt and pepper to taste.
2. While the meat cooks, chop the pickles into a fine dice. Peel the red onion and cut it into thin rounds.
3. Cut the core out of the iceberg lettuce and shred the leaves into manageable, bite-sized pieces.
4. Prepare the dressing by whisking together the mayonnaise, ketchup, a dash of salt, pepper, and paprika. Thin the mixture with a small splash of water if necessary to achieve a smooth, pourable consistency.
5. Distribute the chopped lettuce among deep serving plates or bowls. Layer each portion with the shredded cheese, warm browned beef, diced pickles, and red onion rings. Finish by generously drizzling the prepared sauce over the top.
6. Serve immediately and bon appétit!
