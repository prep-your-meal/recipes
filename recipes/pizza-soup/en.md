## Preparation

1. Begin by finely dicing the onion. Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a pot and sweat the onion until it becomes soft and translucent.
2. Incorporate the ground beef into the pot, cooking and stirring for approximately 5 minutes until browned.
3. While the meat browns, chop the bell pepper, salami, and mushrooms. Toss these vegetables and cured meat into the pot with the beef and cook briefly.
4. Pour in the vegetable broth to deglaze the pot, scraping up any browned bits, and allow the mixture to simmer gently for about 10 minutes.
5. Whisk in the strained tomatoes, heavy cream, and mascarpone cheese, stirring continuously until the base is completely smooth and well incorporated.
6. Season the mixture to taste with salt, pepper, and dried oregano, then allow it to come to a brief boil.
7. Ladle the hot soup into bowls and garnish each portion with a spoonful of crème fraîche and a generous dusting of grated Parmesan.
8. Bon appétit!
