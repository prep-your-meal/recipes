## Preparation

1. Begin by prepping the aromatics: remove the skins from the onion and garlic. Cut the onion in half from root to stem, then slice one half and finely dice the remaining half.
2. Warm the rapeseed oil in a skillet over medium heat. Toss in the diced onion and let it sweat and soften for roughly 2 minutes.
3. Mince the garlic directly into the pan using a garlic press, followed by the chili powder and cumin. Combine everything thoroughly and cook for an additional minute to toast the spices.
4. Introduce the ground beef to the skillet. Break the meat apart with a utensil and brown it for about 5 minutes, seasoning with salt and pepper to taste.
5. While the meat cooks, prepare the fresh toppings: slice the romaine lettuce into thin ribbons, chop the cilantro leaves finely, and dice the tomatoes.
6. Assemble the dish by dividing the shredded lettuce among serving bowls. Layer each portion with the cooked beef, shredded cheddar, diced tomatoes, raw sliced onion, and fresh cilantro.
7. Whisk the chili sauce with a splash of water to achieve a lighter consistency, then drizzle it generously over each bowl before serving.
