title: "Chicken Coconut Curry with Zucchini"
---

## Preparation

1. Begin by removing the onion's outer skin and chopping it into small, uniform cubes.
2. Slice the chicken breasts into manageable, bite-sized portions, then place them in a skillet along with the prepared onion and coconut oil, cooking until lightly browned.
3. While the poultry browns, rinse the zucchini thoroughly and cut it into neat dice.
4. Toss the zucchini into the skillet with the chicken and onions, allowing the mixture to cook over medium heat for approximately 3 minutes before pouring in the coconut milk to deglaze the pan.
5. Incorporate your chosen seasonings into the sauce, stirring well, and bring the entire mixture to a gentle, brief boil.
6. Divide the finished dish among individual serving plates and enjoy immediately.
