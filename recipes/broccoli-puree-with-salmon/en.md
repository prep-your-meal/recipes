---

## Preparation

1. Begin by peeling the potatoes and cutting them into rough chunks. Transfer them to a saucepan, add cold salted water just until submerged, and bring to a boil. Lower the heat to medium and let them simmer for roughly 20 minutes until fork-tender.
2. In the meantime, break the broccoli down into individual florets. Place them into a separate pot of water, bring to a boil, and cook at a gentle simmer for about 20 minutes.
3. While the vegetables are cooking, peel and finely mince the onion. Heat one-third of the olive oil in a skillet and cook the onion for approximately 4 minutes until softened. Finely chop the fresh parsley and set aside.
4. Once tender, drain both the potatoes and the broccoli thoroughly. Combine them in a vessel along with the cooked onions, vegetable broth, a pinch of salt and pepper, and half of the remaining olive oil. Mash or blend the mixture until completely smooth and creamy, then cover to keep warm.
5. In a separate skillet, warm the rest of the olive oil over medium heat. Place the salmon fillets in the pan and sear for about 4 minutes on each side.
6. While the fish is searing, peel and finely grate the garlic. In a compact mixing bowl, whisk the garlic together with the honey and soy sauce.
7. Drizzle the prepared glaze directly over the salmon in the skillet, allowing it to bubble and simmer for about 1 minute until fragrant and slightly reduced.
8. Plate the warm broccoli and potato mash, lay the glazed salmon fillets right on top, sprinkle with the freshly chopped parsley, and serve immediately.
