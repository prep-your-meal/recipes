## Preparation

1. Start by peeling the potatoes and cutting them into quarters. Transfer them to a pot and pour in enough cold water to submerge them completely.
2. Put a lid on the pot and bring the water to a rolling boil. Afterward, lower the heat and let the potatoes gently simmer for approximately 20 minutes until tender.
3. While the potatoes are cooking, prep your vegetables by finely mincing the garlic, dicing the bell pepper, and chopping the mushrooms.
4. When the potatoes are finished, warm the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the prepared vegetables and sauté them for roughly 4 minutes.
5. Pour the diced tomatoes into the pan to deglaze it, scraping up any browned bits, and season the mixture with salt and pepper to taste.
6. Allow the sauce and vegetables to simmer together for about 5 minutes before plating alongside the cooked potatoes.
