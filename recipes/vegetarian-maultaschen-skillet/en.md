## Preparation

1. Submerge the Maultaschen in simmering water and cook for roughly 4 minutes, then gently strain.
2. While the pasta cooks, chop the mushrooms into cubes, cut the bell peppers into manageable, bite-sized portions, and slice the spring onions into thin rounds.
3. Strip the skin from the garlic clove and mince it finely.
4. Cut the drained Maultaschen into individual slices.
5. Warm a portion of the olive oil in a spacious skillet over medium-high heat, add the Maultaschen pieces, and sear for approximately 5 minutes until golden brown on every surface.
6. Take the seared Maultaschen out of the pan and reserve them on a plate.
7. Pour the remaining olive oil into the same skillet, tossing in the spring onions and minced garlic to sweat over medium heat for about 2 minutes.
8. Incorporate the chopped mushrooms and bell pepper chunks into the aromatics, cooking for an additional 5 minutes.
9. Transfer the Maultaschen back into the skillet, pour in the vegetable broth, sprinkle with salt and pepper to taste, and allow the mixture to gently bubble for 5 minutes.
10. As the dish simmers, mince the chives, blending the majority into the sour cream while keeping a small portion aside for the final plating.
11. Plate the warm Maultaschen skillet, crown each portion with a dollop of the seasoned sour cream, and scatter the reserved chives over the top as a garnish.
