---
title: "Cucumber Salad with Crème Fraîche and Chives"
---

## Preparation

1. Begin by slicing the cucumbers and finely mincing the chives into petite rings. Remember to reserve a small portion of the chives to use as a garnish later.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the sliced cucumbers and the bulk of the chives together with the crème fraîche, seasoning with salt and pepper to taste.
3. Slice the lemon in half; juice one of those halves directly into the creamy cucumber mixture. Cut the other half into wedges for serving.
4. Plate the salad, top with the reserved chives, and accompany with the fresh lemon wedges.
5. Serve and enjoy your dish!
