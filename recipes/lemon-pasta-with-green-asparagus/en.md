title: "Lemon Pasta with Green Asparagus"
---

## Preparation

1. Fill a large pot with water, bring it to a rolling boil, and prepare the tagliatelle following the package directions.
2. While the pasta cooks, snap off the tough ends of the asparagus spears and chop the remaining stalks into bite-sized lengths.
3. Peel the cloves of garlic and slice them into thin rounds.
4. Warm one-third of the total olive oil in a skillet, add the asparagus, and sear over high heat for roughly 5 minutes until tender-crisp.
5. Stir in the sliced garlic and cook for 1 additional minute. Take the skillet off the heat and transfer the asparagus and garlic to a separate bowl.
6. Zest the lemon to collect the skin's outer layer, then squeeze the juice from the fruit.
7. Use a grater to shred the Parmesan cheese and finely chop the fresh basil leaves.
8. Whisk together the lemon juice, grated Parmesan, the rest of the olive oil, chopped basil, salt, and pepper in a mixing bowl.
9. Before draining the tagliatelle, scoop out and reserve a small amount of the starchy cooking water. Drain the pasta thoroughly.
10. Return the pasta to the skillet, drizzle in the reserved pasta water along with the cheese dressing, and toss over heat for about 2 minutes.
11. Fold the cooked asparagus back into the skillet, warm everything through for 1 more minute, and top with the fresh lemon zest before serving.
12. Enjoy your meal!
