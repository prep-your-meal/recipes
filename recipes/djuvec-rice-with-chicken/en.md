## Preparation

1. Slice the chicken breasts into strips. Peel the onions and garlic, then mince them finely.
2. Remove the core and seeds from the bell pepper, dicing it into small pieces. Peel the carrot and dice it uniformly alongside the zucchini.
3. Warm the olive oil in a spacious skillet over medium heat. Sear the chicken strips for roughly 5 minutes until nicely browned, season with salt and pepper to taste, and remove from the pan, setting them aside.
4. Using the same skillet, sweat the minced onions and garlic for about 60 seconds. Concurrently, rinse the rice thoroughly under cold water.
5. Incorporate the rinsed rice, diced bell pepper, carrot, zucchini, ajvar, and tomato paste directly into the skillet, stirring and frying for another minute.
6. Pour in the canned diced tomatoes and the vegetable broth. Season with paprika, salt, and pepper, then cover and let the mixture simmer over medium heat for approximately 10 minutes.
7. Stir in the green peas and the reserved chicken. Cover again and let everything simmer for an additional 10 minutes, or until the rice is tender and has fully soaked up the cooking liquid.
8. Serve hot and enjoy!
