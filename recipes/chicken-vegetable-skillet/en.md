---

## Method

1. Begin by slicing the chicken breasts into small, bite-sized portions.
2. Mince the onions finely, then sweat them in a skillet with a drizzle of olive oil until translucent.
3. Drop the chicken pieces into the skillet with the onions and brown them evenly on all sides.
4. Meanwhile, chop the carrots, zucchini, and red bell peppers into small dice. Toss these vegetables into a separate, dry pan and roast them without any oil for roughly 7 minutes.
5. Pour the canned crushed tomatoes directly into the roasted vegetables and bring the mixture to a gentle boil.
6. Transfer the cooked chicken and onions into the tomato and vegetable sauce, season with salt and pepper according to your preference, and dish it up while hot.
