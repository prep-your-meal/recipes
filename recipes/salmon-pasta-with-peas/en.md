## Preparation

1. Defrost the frozen peas ahead of time.
2. Fill a pot with water, bring it to a rolling boil, and cook the whole grain pasta for roughly 12 minutes until it reaches an al dente texture.
3. While the pasta is cooking, peel and thinly slice the garlic, chop the fresh dill, and slice the smoked salmon into neat strips.
4. Warm the rapeseed oil in a skillet over medium heat, then add the garlic and cook for about 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Whisk the crème fraîche into the skillet until smooth, gently fold in the salmon strips, and allow the mixture to simmer for roughly 2 minutes.
6. Stir in the thawed peas, season to taste with salt, black pepper, and the freshly chopped dill, and continue to simmer for an additional 2 minutes.
7. Strain the cooked pasta thoroughly, divide it among serving plates, and generously spoon the warm salmon sauce over the top.
8. Serve immediately and enjoy!
