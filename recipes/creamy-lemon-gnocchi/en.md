title: "Creamy Lemon Gnocchi"
---

## Preparation

1. Cook the gnocchi following the directions on the package until done.
2. Place the pine nuts in an ungreased skillet over medium heat, toasting them until they achieve a rich, golden color and aromatic scent.
3. While the gnocchi cooks, use a microplane to harvest the lemon zest, reserving it for later. Extract the fresh lemon juice into a mixing bowl, then whisk it together with the crème fraîche, seasoning generously with salt and pepper to form a smooth sauce.
4. Combine the warm gnocchi thoroughly with the prepared sauce. Finish the dish by scattering the toasted pine nuts, vibrant lemon zest, and a generous dusting of grated Parmesan over the top.
5. Serve immediately and savor your culinary creation!
