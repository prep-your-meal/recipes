## Preparation

1. Boil the pasta following the package directions until al dente.
2. While the pasta cooks, prepare the sauce by blending together the strained tomatoes, cottage cheese, most of the grated parmesan (reserve a portion for topping), olive oil, chili flakes, salt, and black pepper.
3. Drain the cooked pasta, making sure to reserve a small amount of the starchy cooking water to mix into the sauce.
4. Mince the fresh parsley finely.
5. Return the pasta to the pot, pour in the prepared sauce along with the reserved cooking water, and toss over medium heat for about one minute until heated through and well combined.
6. Plate the dish and garnish with the freshly chopped parsley and the reserved parmesan cheese.
7. Serve immediately and enjoy your meal!
