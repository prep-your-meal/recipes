---

## Preparation

1. Begin by removing the skins from both the onion and the garlic, then finely chop the onion.
2. Warm the coconut oil in a skillet, toss in the chopped onion, and mince the garlic directly into the pan. Cook this mixture for roughly 2 minutes.
3. Incorporate the ginger, curry powder, lentils, and sunflower seeds, stirring thoroughly to combine all the ingredients.
4. Pour in the vegetable broth and coconut milk to deglaze the pan, reduce the heat to medium, and allow the mixture to gently simmer for approximately 25 minutes. Finish by adjusting the seasoning with salt and pepper to taste.
5. Serve and enjoy!
