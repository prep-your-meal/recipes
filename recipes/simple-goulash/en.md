## Preparation

1. Trim the beef stew meat down into convenient, bite-sized portions if required.
2. Finely chop the onion and sweat it in a spacious pot using rapeseed oil for roughly 3 minutes until translucent.
3. Introduce the beef to the pot and brown it aggressively over high heat for an additional 3 minutes.
4. Stir in the tomato paste along with a single cup of water, allow the mixture to reduce slightly, and season generously with salt, black pepper, and paprika.
5. Pour in the rest of the water, lower the heat, and let the contents simmer gently for 45 minutes.
6. While the base cooks, prepare the potatoes by peeling them; finely grate one potato to act as a natural thickening agent, and chop the remaining potatoes into bite-sized cubes.
7. Transfer all the potatoes into the pot, mix thoroughly, put the lid on, and continue to simmer on low heat for another 45 minutes until tender.
8. Plate up hot and savor your meal!
