## Preparation

1. Dice the chicken breasts into bite-sized portions, season generously with salt and pepper, and sear them in an ungreased skillet for roughly 8 minutes until thoroughly cooked.
2. While the chicken cooks, thinly slice the spring onions.
3. Transfer the cooked chicken to a separate bowl, then briefly sauté the spring onions in olive oil for about 2 minutes.
4. Pour the vegetable broth into the pan along with the dry brown rice, put the lid on, and allow the mixture to simmer for approximately 15 minutes.
5. In the meantime, break the broccoli down into bite-sized florets. Uncover the pan when the rice is halfway done, mix in the broccoli, and continue cooking uncovered.
6. To finish, fold the grated cheddar cheese and the reserved chicken back into the skillet, letting everything simmer together for a final 2 minutes until the cheese is melted and incorporated.
