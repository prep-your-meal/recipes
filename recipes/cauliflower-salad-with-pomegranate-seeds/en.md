## Preparation

1. Break the cauliflower apart into smaller florets, then process them on a fine grater until fine.
2. Separate the seeds from the pomegranate (hint: submerging the fruit in a bowl of water prevents the juice from splattering).
3. Place the slivered almonds into an ungreased skillet and toast them over medium heat until they achieve a golden-brown hue and nutty aroma.
4. Mince the mint leaves finely, collect roughly 1 teaspoon of lemon zest, and extract the fresh juice from the lemon.
5. Prepare the dressing by whisking the olive oil, water, almond butter, lemon juice, lemon zest, ras el hanout, turmeric, ginger, salt, and black pepper together in a bowl until smooth.
6. Toss the grated cauliflower, pomegranate arils, toasted almonds, and chopped mint gently with the prepared dressing until everything is evenly coated, then dish up and enjoy.
