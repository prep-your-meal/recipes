title: "Quinoa Salad with Grilled Chicken"
---

## Preparation

1. Heat a small amount of olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken breast fillets and sear on both sides until golden brown and fully cooked through.
2. In a small saucepan, combine the quinoa and vegetable broth. Bring the mixture to a simmer and cook for approximately 15 minutes, or until the liquid is absorbed and the grain is tender.
3. While the quinoa is simmering, prep your produce: dice the tomatoes and cucumber, and thinly slice the spring onions.
4. Transfer the cooked quinoa to a mixing bowl and toss with the diced vegetables, fresh parsley, and a drizzle of olive oil. Season the mixture to taste with salt and pepper.
5. Cut the warm chicken breasts into neat slices, lay them over the prepared quinoa salad, and serve immediately.
