## Preparation

1. Submerge the spinach in a pot of boiling salted water and let it blanch for about 2 minutes.
2. Remove the spinach, dry it thoroughly with paper towels, and finely dice it.
3. Clean the chicken breasts under cold water, pat them dry with paper towels, and carefully slice them horizontally almost all the way through to create a pocket (butterfly cut).
4. Slice the mozzarella ball into thin rounds, then stuff the pocket of each chicken breast with the prepared spinach and mozzarella slices.
5. Crack and whisk the egg in a shallow bowl. Dip the stuffed chicken cutlets into the beaten egg, press them into breadcrumbs until fully coated on all sides, and arrange them on a lined baking sheet.
6. Place the baking sheet in a preheated oven at 175°C (350°F) and bake for 25 minutes until cooked through and golden.
7. Serve and enjoy your meal!
