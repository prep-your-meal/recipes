title: "Korean Cucumber Salad"
---

## Preparation

1. Begin by cutting the cucumber down the center lengthwise, then slice each half into thin half-moons.
2. Remove the skin from the garlic cloves and mince them finely, then cut the scallion crosswise into delicate rings.
3. Bring all the prepared components together in a bowl, toss well to distribute the flavors evenly, and present immediately as a flavorful accompaniment.
4. Bon appétit!
