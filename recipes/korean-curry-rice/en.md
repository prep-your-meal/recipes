## Preparation

1. Prepare your rice according to the package directions.
2. While the rice is cooking, remove the skins from the potatoes and onions. Chop the potatoes, onions, pork tenderloin, and carrot into equal, bite-sized cubes.
3. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat, then add the pork tenderloin and brown for roughly 3 minutes. Toss in the potato cubes and continue to cook for an additional 3 minutes.
4. Incorporate the carrot and onion pieces into the pot, cooking them together for a short moment until slightly softened.
5. Measure out 3 tablespoons of water and set it aside in a separate small container. Pour the rest of the water into the saucepan, place a lid on top, and allow the mixture to gently simmer for approximately 10 minutes.
6. In the meantime, whisk the curry powder into the reserved water until smooth.
7. Pour the curry paste into the pot, stir thoroughly to ensure all ingredients are evenly coated, and dish up hot alongside your freshly prepared rice.
