## Preparation

1. Prepare the rice following the package guidelines.
2. Break the broccoli down into bite-sized florets, rinse thoroughly, and drop them into a pot of boiling water.
3. Allow the broccoli to cook for 4 minutes, then immediately drain and flush with cold water to stop the cooking process.
4. While the broccoli is boiling, mince the garlic and chili pepper, then gently sauté them in olive oil using a spacious skillet.
5. Slice the spring onions into thin rounds, toss them into the skillet alongside the blanched broccoli, and cook for an additional 3 minutes.
6. Cut the cherry tomatoes in half and incorporate them into the pan along with the cooked rice, stirring continuously as everything heats through.
7. Break the salmon apart into flakes using a fork, gently warm it through in the skillet with the other ingredients, and season the entire mixture with salt and pepper to taste.
8. Drizzle in the soy sauce, toss everything together until evenly combined, and divide among serving dishes.
9. Enjoy!
