## Preparation

1. Warm your oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Clean the potatoes thoroughly, dry them completely with a towel, and slice them into evenly sized wedges.
3. Coat the wedges with one-third of the total olive oil, then season generously with salt and pepper.
4. Spread the potatoes out in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet and roast on the center oven rack for roughly half an hour.
5. While the potatoes are in the oven, chop the chicken breast into bite-sized portions and toss them with half of what remains of the olive oil.
6. Extract the juice from the lemon. Peel the garlic cloves and mince them finely.
7. Season the chicken with half the lemon juice, half the minced garlic, dried oregano, salt, and pepper. Toss to coat, then fry in a skillet over medium heat for about 8 minutes until fully cooked.
8. Prepare the sauce by combining the Greek yogurt in a mixing bowl with the leftover lemon juice, the rest of the garlic, the remaining olive oil, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
9. Grate the first half of the cucumber finely, press out any excess liquid using a clean towel or paper towels, and stir it into the yogurt mixture. Set your finished tzatziki aside.
10. Chop the tomato into wedges, slice the remaining half of the cucumber, peel and slice the red onion into thin half-moons, and tear the lettuce leaves into manageable pieces.
11. Divide the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber slices, red onion, cooked chicken, and roasted potato wedges among serving bowls, and accompany each with a generous dollop of the homemade tzatziki.
