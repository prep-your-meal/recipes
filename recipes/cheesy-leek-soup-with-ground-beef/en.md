title: "Cheesy Leek Soup with Ground Beef"
---

## Preparation

1. Trim and finely mince the spring onions, keeping the white and green parts separate.
2. Warm a splash of olive oil in a skillet, then sweat the minced onions for approximately 3 minutes until softened.
3. Introduce the ground beef to the pan, breaking it up as it cooks, and brown for roughly 5 minutes. Season generously with salt and pepper to taste.
4. While the meat browns, prep the leek: discard the root end and dark green tops, slice it down the middle, rinse thoroughly to remove any grit, and chop into small dice.
5. Toss the prepared leek into the skillet and cook for another 3 minutes. Pour in the vegetable broth, scraping the bottom of the pan to release any browned bits.
6. Blend in the processed cheese, lower the heat to medium, and allow the soup to gently simmer for 10 to 15 minutes.
7. Ladle into bowls and serve warm!
