title: "One-Pot Broccoli Pasta"
---

## Preparation

1. Begin by prepping your vegetables: slice the mushrooms, break the broccoli into bite-sized florets, and rinse the fresh spinach leaves, setting them aside for later.
2. Transfer the sliced mushrooms, whole-grain pasta, vegetable broth, and cream into a large pot. Turn the heat to high until the liquid reaches a boil, then reduce the heat and let it gently simmer for roughly 5 minutes.
3. Incorporate the broccoli florets into the pot and continue simmering for an additional 5 minutes.
4. Fold in the prepared spinach, season the dish with salt and pepper according to your preference, and cook for 2 final minutes.
5. Plate your meal immediately and savor!
