## Preparation

1. Rub the ground beef with a pinch of salt and fry it in a skillet using a tablespoon of olive oil until well browned.
2. While the meat cooks, finely mince the onion and garlic, transfer them to a separate saucepan, and sweat them until they turn soft and translucent.
3. Incorporate the browned beef, strained tomatoes, and fresh basil into the saucepan, season to taste with salt and pepper, and allow the sauce to gently simmer over low heat for 35 minutes.
4. While the sauce bubbles away, slice the zucchini, sprinkle salt on both sides of the pieces, and let them rest for 10 minutes.
5. Once the salt has successfully drawn out the excess moisture, use paper towels to thoroughly pat the zucchini slices dry.
6. Transfer the dried vegetable rounds to a hot skillet and sear them quickly on each side.
7. In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta, the egg, and two-thirds of the grated mozzarella, stirring until completely uniform.
8. Coat the bottom of an 18.5 x 18.5 cm baking dish with a layer of the meat sauce, spoon over a portion of the cheese filling, and arrange a layer of seared zucchini on top.
9. Continue building the dish by repeating these layers until all of the prepared components have been used.
10. Top the assembled lasagna with the remaining shredded mozzarella, cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil, and bake in an oven preheated to 180°C (350°F) for 20 minutes.
11. Peel off the foil and continue baking for an additional 10 minutes to achieve a golden, bubbly crust.
12. Serve and enjoy!
