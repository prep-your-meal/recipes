## Preparation

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil and prepare the macaroni following the package guidelines.
2. While the pasta cooks, peel the onion, then finely dice it alongside the red bell pepper and carrots.
3. Prepare the avocados by cutting them in half, removing the pits, and peeling away the skin. Roughly chop the flesh and place it into a food processor. Add the lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper, then process until the mixture is completely smooth and creamy.
4. Once the macaroni is done, drain it thoroughly and combine it in a large bowl with the diced vegetables and the creamy avocado dressing, tossing until everything is evenly coated.
5. Plate immediately and dig in!
