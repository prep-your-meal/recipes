---

## Preparation

1. Begin by chopping the sweet potatoes into uniform, bite-sized pieces.
2. Warm the olive oil in a spacious skillet over medium heat, then cook the sweet potato cubes for roughly 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Thoroughly rinse the kale, toss it into the skillet, and combine it well with the softening sweet potatoes.
4. Incorporate the diced tomatoes and chickpeas into the pan, stirring thoroughly to integrate all the ingredients.
5. Pour the uncooked brown rice and vegetable broth directly into the skillet. Season with salt and pepper to taste, cover with a lid, and allow the mixture to simmer for approximately 25 minutes, making sure to stir from time to time.
6. Serve hot and enjoy!
