## Preparation

1. Begin by peeling the potatoes and cutting them into quarters, then transfer them to a saucepan.
2. Pour in enough cold water to completely submerge the potatoes and season generously with salt.
3. Put the lid on, bring the water to a boil, and let it simmer gently for roughly 20 minutes until tender.
4. While the potatoes cook, finely dice the shallots and slice the mushrooms.
5. Cut the carrots into rounds, then quarter each round.
6. Warm the olive oil in a spacious pot over medium heat and cook the shallots for approximately 3 minutes until softened.
7. Toss in the carrots, mushrooms, and peas, continuing to cook and stir for about 5 minutes.
8. Incorporate the tomato paste, flour, vegetable broth, salt, and rosemary, stirring thoroughly to combine. Allow the mixture to simmer for about 10 minutes, remembering to stir periodically.
9. While the vegetables simmer, set your oven to preheat to 180°C (350°F).
10. Once the potatoes are done, drain away the cooking water and mash them thoroughly using a potato masher or hand mixer.
11. Gently blend the Greek yogurt into the warm mashed potatoes until smooth and creamy.
12. Spoon the cooked vegetable filling into a baking dish and top it evenly with the creamy mashed potatoes.
13. Place the dish on the middle rack of the preheated oven and bake for about 20 minutes until golden and bubbling.
14. Serve hot and enjoy!
