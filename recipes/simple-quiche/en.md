## Preparation

1. In a mixing bowl, combine the wheat flour, margarine, water, and salt, working the ingredients by hand until a cohesive and smooth dough comes together.
2. Transfer the dough to the refrigerator and allow it to chill for approximately 30 minutes.
3. As the resting time nears its end, use a hand mixer to beat the eggs in a separate bowl until they achieve a creamy consistency. Incorporate the cooking cream, oat drink, salt, nutmeg, and cornstarch, blending thoroughly until uniform.
4. Warm your oven up to 200°C (390°F).
5. Evenly press the chilled dough across the bottom and up the sides of an 18 cm (Ø) springform pan.
6. Distribute the diced ham uniformly over the pastry base, then carefully pour the prepared egg and oat mixture on top.
7. Place the quiche on the center oven rack and bake for roughly 45 minutes. Should the surface begin to color too rapidly, shield it with a sheet of aluminum foil.
8. Serve and savor your creation!
