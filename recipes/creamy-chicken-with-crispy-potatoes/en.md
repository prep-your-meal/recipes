## Preparation

1. Warm your oven to 200 °C (390 °F) using the conventional top and bottom heat setting.
2. In a compact mixing bowl, combine the garlic powder, Italian seasoning, chili flakes, paprika, salt, and freshly ground black pepper to create your spice blend.
3. Dice the potatoes into bite-sized cubes. Toss them thoroughly with half of the olive oil, half of the prepared spice mixture, and the onion powder.
4. Arrange the seasoned potato cubes in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking tray. Roast on the center oven rack for roughly 25 minutes, or until nicely golden and crisp, remembering to turn them over halfway through the cooking time.
5. While the potatoes are roasting, cut the chicken breast fillets into neat dice. Coat them with the remaining olive oil and the other half of the spice blend, then cook in a skillet over medium heat for approximately 5 minutes, stirring periodically.
6. Incorporate the parmesan cheese, cream cheese, and milk directly into the chicken. Stir thoroughly and let the mixture gently simmer for an additional 3 minutes.
7. While the sauce simmers, finely slice the scallions into delicate rings.
8. Transfer the roasted, crispy potato cubes straight into the skillet with the creamy chicken mixture and toss gently to combine.
9. To finish, fold in the mozzarella cheese, stirring continuously until it melts into a rich, stretchy consistency. Portion the dish into bowls and garnish with the freshly sliced scallions before serving.
