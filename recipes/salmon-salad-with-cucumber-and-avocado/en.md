title: "Salmon Salad with Cucumber and Avocado"
---

## Preparation

1. Chop the salmon fillet into uniform, bite-sized pieces. Season them lightly with salt and pepper, arrange them on a parchment-lined baking sheet, and bake at 180°C (350°F) until the edges are nicely browned.
2. While the fish is in the oven, thoroughly rinse the mixed salad greens and place them into a roomy serving bowl.
3. Remove the avocado pit, peel the flesh, and cut it into thin slices. Do the same with the radishes and the cucumber.
4. Take the roasted salmon out of the oven and add the warm cubes directly into the bowl with the greens and vegetables.
5. In a separate small container, vigorously blend the fresh lemon juice, olive oil, and a pinch of salt and pepper until emulsified, then pour this dressing evenly over the salad.
6. Sprinkle with sesame seeds as a finishing touch and bring immediately to the table.
