## Preparation

1. **Prep Ingredients:** Chop the chicken breast into manageable, bite-sized cubes and slice the bell pepper into strips.
2. **Aromatics:** Warm a drizzle of oil in a wok or skillet, incorporate the red curry paste, and stir-fry for roughly 60 seconds until wonderfully aromatic.
3. **Protein & Liquids:** Toss in the chicken chunks, searing them quickly on every side before pouring in the coconut milk.
4. **Simmer:** Introduce the bell pepper to the pan, lower the heat, and allow the mixture to gently bubble for about 15 minutes until the poultry is thoroughly cooked through and the sauce achieves a rich, reduced consistency.
5. **Plate Up:** Enjoy immediately while hot, paired ideally with a side of fragrant jasmine rice.
