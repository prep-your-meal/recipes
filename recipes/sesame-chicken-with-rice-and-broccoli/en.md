## Preparation

1. Prepare the brown rice following the package directions.
2. While the rice is cooking, break the broccoli down into bite-sized florets and boil them in a pot of water for 7 to 8 minutes until tender.
3. Slice the chicken breasts into manageable pieces and cook them in a non-stick skillet using a splash of water.
4. Whisk together the honey, soy sauce, water, cornstarch, ginger, and red pepper flakes in a small container to create the sauce.
5. Pour the sauce directly into the skillet with the chicken, reducing the heat and simmering for roughly 10 minutes until the meat is thoroughly coated and infused with the flavors.
6. Plate the meal by combining the rice, broccoli, and glazed chicken, then finish the dish with a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
