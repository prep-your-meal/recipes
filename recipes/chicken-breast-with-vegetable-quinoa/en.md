title: "Chicken Breast with Vegetable Quinoa"
---

## Method

1. Prepare the quinoa following the package guidelines, and allow the frozen broccoli to thaw slightly.
2. Warm a drizzle of olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Brown the chicken breasts on both sides until a golden crust forms, then remove them from the pan and reserve.
3. While the chicken cooks, core and deseed the bell peppers, slicing them into delicate, thin ribbons.
4. Toss the broccoli and bell pepper strips into the same skillet, covering them to steam gently for roughly 7 minutes until tender-crisp.
5. Combine the warm, tender vegetables with the prepared quinoa, adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper to taste, and plate alongside the golden chicken breasts.
