## Preparation

1. Place the dried mushrooms in a bowl, cover with hot water, and let them rehydrate.
2. Mince the peeled garlic. Core the bell pepper, then cut it and the zucchini in half before slicing them uniformly.
3. Trim the stem off the broccoli and break the head down into bite-sized florets.
4. Prepare the mie noodles by following the package directions.
5. Warm the coconut oil in a pot over a medium flame, then sweat the garlic, ginger, and curry paste for roughly 60 seconds until fragrant.
6. Pour in the vegetable broth and coconut milk, bring the mixture to a boil for a minute, then lower the heat. Whisk in the maple syrup, soy sauce, and peanut butter until smooth.
7. Allow the broth to gently simmer for about 10 minutes. Strain the mushrooms, and toss them into the pot alongside the bell pepper, zucchini, and broccoli, cooking for an additional 5 minutes.
8. Fold the cooked mie noodles directly into the soup. Cut the lime into quarters and serve alongside the bowls for a fresh citrus garnish.
