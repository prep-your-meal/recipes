title: "Tomato and Corn Salad"
---

## Preparation

1. Begin by dicing the cucumber, quartering the tomatoes, slicing the spring onions into thin rings, and thoroughly draining the canned corn.
2. Transfer the prepared cucumber, tomatoes, spring onions, and corn into a mixing bowl, then toss them together with the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, and ajvar until well combined.
3. Finish the dish by seasoning with ground cumin and salt to your personal preference, give it one final stir, and plate immediately.
4. Bon appétit!
