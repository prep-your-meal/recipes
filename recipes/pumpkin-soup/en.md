---

## Preparation

1. Scoop out the pumpkin seeds and chop the flesh into chunky pieces.
2. Peel the potatoes, onion, and ginger, then roughly dice them.
3. Place a pot on the heat, drizzle in the rapeseed oil, and gently sweat the onion for a short moment.
4. Toss in the rest of the chopped vegetables and sauté the mixture for roughly 3 minutes.
5. Pour in the vegetable broth and the coconut milk, making sure to reserve 2 tablespoons of the coconut milk to use as a garnish later.
6. Bring the contents to a simmer over medium heat and cook for 10 minutes.
7. While the soup is cooking, remove the apple cores and dice the fruit, stirring the pieces into the pot once the initial 10-minute timer is up.
8. Continue simmering the soup for an additional 10 minutes, then process with an immersion blender until completely silky and smooth.
9. Adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper as needed, ladle into bowls, and finish each portion with a swirl of the reserved coconut milk.
