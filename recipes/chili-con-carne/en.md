## Preparation

1. Begin by draining the kidney beans. Next, dice your tomatoes, slice the carrots into thin rounds, and finely mince the fresh cilantro.
2. Mince the onion, garlic, and chili pepper as finely as possible.
3. Warm the coconut oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the carrots, onion, garlic, and chili, cooking them briefly until they begin to soften. Toss in the ground beef and cook, stirring frequently, until it is thoroughly browned, which should take about 5 minutes.
4. Pour in the diced tomatoes, water, and the prepared kidney beans to deglaze the pan. Lower the heat to medium and let the mixture simmer gently for 15 minutes.
5. Stir in the freshly grated ginger, then taste the dish and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper as needed.
6. Plate your dish and sprinkle the minced cilantro generously over the top.
7. Serve hot and enjoy!
