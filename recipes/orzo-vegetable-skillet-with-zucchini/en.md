## Preparation

1. Begin by removing the skins from the onion and the garlic, and proceed to mince the onion finely.
2. Warm the olive oil in a spacious skillet over medium heat, then introduce the chopped onion. Mince the garlic directly into the pan using a garlic press, and incorporate the tomato paste. Allow this mixture to cook down, stirring frequently, for approximately 3 minutes.
3. Pour the orzo pasta and the vegetable broth into the skillet. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer, cooking for about 10 minutes while stirring every now and then.
4. While the pasta is simmering, prepare the produce: slice the zucchini lengthwise, then cut into quarters, and quarter the cherry tomatoes as well. Finely mince the fresh parsley.
5. Toss the zucchini chunks and quartered tomatoes into the skillet with the orzo. Season the dish with salt, black pepper, and dried basil, letting everything cook together for another 5 minutes.
6. Gently stir the chopped fresh parsley into the finished dish just before plating and serving.
