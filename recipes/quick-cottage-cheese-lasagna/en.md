---

## Preparation

1. Set your oven's temperature to 175°C (350°F) so it heats up properly.
2. Warm a drizzle of olive oil in a skillet set over medium heat, then add the ground beef, cooking for roughly 5 minutes until it is thoroughly browned.
3. While the meat browns, peel and finely chop the garlic, blend it with the strained tomatoes, and incorporate this mixture into the cooked beef.
4. Grab a mixing bowl and combine the cottage cheese, grated Parmesan cheese, and Italian herbs.
5. In your baking dish, lay down one-third of the meat sauce as the base, place half of the lasagna noodles over it, and spread half of the cottage cheese filling evenly across the noodles.
6. Build the next layer using half of the leftover meat sauce, followed by the remaining lasagna sheets and the rest of the cottage cheese mixture, finishing this sequence with the final portion of meat sauce on top.
7. Cut the mozzarella into slices and arrange them uniformly across the surface of the lasagna.
8. Place the dish on the center oven rack and bake for approximately 30 minutes.
