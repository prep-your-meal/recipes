## Preparation

1. Warm your oven to 200°C (390°F).
2. Chop the potatoes into manageable, bite-sized chunks and break the broccoli apart into individual florets.
3. Fill a spacious pot with water, bring it to a boil, and let the potatoes simmer over medium heat for 5 minutes. Toss in the broccoli florets and continue cooking both vegetables together for an additional 5 minutes.
4. While the vegetables cook, bring the vegetable broth to a boil in a separate small saucepan for one minute. Take it off the heat, stir in the cream cheese, and whisk vigorously until the mixture is completely smooth.
5. In a small container, whisk the flour and milk together until combined, then stir this mixture into the sauce. Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer, stirring constantly for two minutes to thicken.
6. Mince the chives finely and stir them into the sauce, along with the curry powder, salt, and pepper.
7. Strain the cooked vegetables thoroughly and arrange them in a baking dish. Pour the prepared sauce evenly over the top, then finish by scattering the shredded cheddar cheese across the surface.
8. Place the dish on the middle rack of the oven and bake for approximately 20 minutes, or until the top is bubbling and beautifully golden brown.
