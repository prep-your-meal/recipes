## Preparation

1. Separate 1 tablespoon of butter into a small dish, and transfer the rest into a large mixing bowl.
2. Incorporate the wheat flour, salt, chilled water, and 50% of the egg yolks into the larger quantity of butter, working the ingredients together manually until a cohesive dough forms.
3. Wrap or cover the dough and allow it to chill in the refrigerator for approximately half an hour.
4. Once the resting time is complete, bring your oven up to a temperature of 180°C (350°F).
5. Give the dough a quick, brief knead, then transfer it to a lightly floured surface and flatten it with a rolling pin until it is roughly 5 mm thick.
6. Coat a quiche dish using a small amount of butter, transfer the pastry into the vessel, press it tightly against the bottom and sides, and create a raised border along the edges.
7. Pierce the bottom surface multiple times using the tines of a fork, then place it in the center of the oven to blind bake for 15 minutes.
8. As the crust bakes, gently melt the reserved tablespoon of butter in a skillet and sauté the diced ham for roughly 3 minutes.
9. Remove the ham from the heat to cool down a bit, shred your cheese, and then combine the ham and cheese together in a bowl.
10. Take the pre-baked quiche shell out of the oven and set it aside to cool down for a short while.
11. While the crust cools, combine the crème fraîche, milk, whole eggs, the remaining egg yolks, black pepper, and salt, whisking thoroughly until smooth.
12. Distribute the ham and cheese blend evenly across the bottom of the pastry crust, followed by a careful pour of the liquid crème fraîche mixture over the top.
13. Return the quiche to the oven and bake for another 40 minutes until set and golden.
14. Enjoy your meal!
