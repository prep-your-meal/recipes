title: "Vegetable Rice with Beef"
---

## Method

1. Begin by preparing the short-grain rice following the directions on the package.
2. While the rice is cooking, thoroughly rinse the fresh produce. Cut the vegetables and the beef into uniform, bite-sized cubes.
3. Warm fifty percent of the sesame oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the bell peppers and carrots for roughly 3 minutes, then stir in the corn and peas just prior to removing them from the heat.
4. Empty the sautéed vegetable mixture into a clean bowl and reserve for later.
5. Season the cubed beef with salt and pepper to taste. Using the rest of the sesame oil, brown the beef in the skillet for approximately 5 minutes.
6. Concurrently, place the sesame seeds in a separate, dry pan and toast them until they achieve a rich, golden color.
7. Combine the cooked rice, vegetables, and beef, tossing thoroughly to blend. Top with freshly chopped parsley before serving.
8. Serve immediately and savor your dish!
