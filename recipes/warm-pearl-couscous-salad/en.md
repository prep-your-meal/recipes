title: "Warm Pearl Couscous Salad"
---

## Preparation

1. Prepare the pearl couscous by following the cooking directions on the package.
2. Cut the tomatoes in half and slice the cucumber into charming half-moon shapes.
3. Warm a quarter of the olive oil in a skillet set over a medium flame, then cook the chickpeas, tomatoes, salt, and pepper together for roughly 6 minutes until tender and flavorful.
4. Prepare the dressing by whisking together the leftover oil, red wine vinegar, ground paprika, and freshly extracted lemon juice in a small bowl.
5. Coarsely chop the fresh mint and basil leaves. In a separate, dry skillet over medium heat, toast the pine nuts until they turn a warm, golden brown.
6. Combine the cooked pearl couscous, the warm chickpea and tomato blend, the sliced cucumber, the chopped herbs, and the prepared dressing in a large bowl, tossing gently to coat.
7. Garnish with the toasted pine nuts and serve immediately.
