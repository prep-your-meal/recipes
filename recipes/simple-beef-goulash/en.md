## Preparation

1. Begin by slicing the beef into manageable, bite-sized portions using a sharp knife.
2. Finely dice the onion and cook it in a large pot with the canola oil over medium heat for approximately 3 minutes until translucent.
3. Introduce the beef to the pot, turning the heat up to high, and sear the meat for another 3 minutes until nicely browned.
4. Stir in a cup of water along with the tomato paste, allow the mixture to reduce slightly, and season generously with salt, pepper, and paprika to your liking.
5. Pour the remaining water into the pot, bring the liquid to a gentle bubble, and let the contents simmer uncovered on low heat for 45 minutes.
6. While the base is simmering, peel the potatoes. Grate a single potato finely—this will act as a natural thickener for your stew—and cut the remaining potatoes into even dice.
7. Once the initial simmering time has passed, mix the diced and grated potatoes into the pot, put the lid on, and continue to simmer on low heat for another 45 minutes until the potatoes are tender and the stew is rich.
