## Preparation

1. Prepare the rice following the directions on the package.
2. While the rice cooks, remove the core from the white cabbage and peel the carrots. Slice both vegetables into delicate, thin strips. Peel the garlic and chop it into a fine mince.
3. Warm half of the sesame oil in a spacious skillet over high heat. Add the ground beef and cook, stirring occasionally, until it is nicely browned (around 5 minutes).
4. As the meat browns, whisk together the ginger, minced garlic, the remaining sesame oil, sweet chili sauce, and soy sauce in a mixing bowl to create your sauce.
5. Toss the sliced cabbage, carrots, and the freshly cooked rice directly into the skillet with the beef, continuing to fry everything together for approximately 4 minutes.
6. Pour in the prepared sauce and add the peas. Stir well and let the mixture cook for another 5 minutes, or until the cabbage reaches your desired tenderness.
7. Finish the dish with a bright splash of lemon juice, then season with salt and pepper to suit your palate.
