title: "Cauliflower in Cheese Sauce"
---

## Preparation

1. Break or slice the cauliflower head into bite-sized florets.
2. Fill a saucepan with water, bring it to a rolling boil, and blanch the florets for roughly 5 minutes until tender-crisp.
3. While the cauliflower cooks, warm the heavy cream in a separate skillet over medium heat, drop in the cream cheese, and whisk until smooth.
4. Reserve a small portion of the cheddar for later use, then stir the rest of the cheddar into the cream mixture until completely melted and incorporated.
5. Once boiled, drain the cauliflower completely to remove excess moisture.
6. Enhance the cheese sauce by mixing in the mustard, turmeric, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
7. Gently fold the drained cauliflower into the spiced cheese sauce until evenly coated, and top with the reserved cheddar as a garnish.
