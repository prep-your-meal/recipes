## Preparation

1. Slice both the white and red cabbages into fine, thin ribbons, keeping them separated in individual mixing bowls.
2. Using your hands, massage each bowl of cabbage vigorously for roughly 3 minutes to tenderize the leaves.
3. Shred the carrot using a grater, then peel and mince or press the garlic clove.
4. Whisk the yogurt, olive oil, apple cider vinegar, maple syrup, minced garlic, salt, and black pepper in a small bowl until smooth and emulsified.
5. Bring the prepared white cabbage, red cabbage, and grated carrot together in a large bowl, pour the dressing over the top, toss thoroughly to coat, and dish up.
6. Serve and enjoy!
