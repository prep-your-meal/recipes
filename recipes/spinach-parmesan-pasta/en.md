title: "Spinach Parmesan Pasta"
---

## Preparation

1. Begin by cooking the whole-wheat spaghetti following the package directions, then strain the pasta thoroughly in a colander.
2. Place the butter back into the same pot over medium heat. Mince or press the garlic cloves and let them gently sauté in the melting butter for approximately 3 minutes until fragrant.
3. Toss the fresh spinach into the pot and cook briefly until it completely wilts.
4. Transfer the drained spaghetti back into the pot, scatter the grated Parmesan cheese evenly over the top, and toss everything together until well combined.
5. Taste the dish and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper as needed.
6. Serve immediately and enjoy!
