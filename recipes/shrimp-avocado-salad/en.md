title: "Shrimp Avocado Salad"
---

## Preparation

1. Extract the juice from the limes, finely dice the onion, and chop the tomatoes into small cubes.
2. Cut the avocados in half to discard the pits, peel away the skin, and slice the flesh into bite-sized chunks.
3. Chop the shrimp into smaller pieces, transfer them to a mixing bowl, and coat them evenly with the freshly extracted lime juice.
4. Rinse and drain the canned black beans and corn, then incorporate them into the bowl alongside the prepared onion, avocado, and tomatoes.
5. Toss the mixture gently to combine all ingredients, adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper according to your preference, and serve immediately.
