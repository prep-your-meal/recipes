---

## Preparation

1. Begin by dicing the chicken breasts into bite-sized pieces. Heat half of the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat, add the chicken, and sauté for roughly 7 minutes until nicely browned and cooked through.
2. Set your oven to heat up to 200°C (390°F).
3. Take the cooked chicken out of the pan. Pour the remaining olive oil into the same pan and fry the gnocchi for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they achieve a golden-brown crust.
4. Season your tomato passata to taste with salt and pepper. Spoon one-third of this sauce into the bottom of a baking dish. Layer in half of the cooked chicken and scatter half of the mozzarella over it. Repeat the process by adding another layer of the remaining sauce, the rest of the chicken, and the golden gnocchi. Finish the assembly by blanketing the top with the remaining mozzarella followed by an even dusting of parmesan cheese.
5. Place the baking dish on the center oven rack and bake for approximately 15 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbling and wonderfully golden. Garnish with freshly torn basil leaves before serving.
6. Serve and enjoy!
