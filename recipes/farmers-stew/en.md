## Preparation

1. Begin by removing the skins from the onion and garlic, then mince both finely.
2. Pull the rosemary needles and thyme leaves away from their woody stems, chop them up finely, and reserve one teaspoon of the mixture to use as a final garnish.
3. Peel the potatoes and cut them into even, bite-sized cubes.
4. Core the bell pepper to remove the seeds, then chop the flesh.
5. Warm the olive oil in a soup pot over medium heat and sear the diced ham for roughly 60 seconds.
6. Toss in the minced onion and garlic, cooking them together for an additional 2 minutes until fragrant.
7. Introduce the ground beef to the pot, breaking it apart with a utensil, and cook for about 5 minutes until it is fully browned and no longer pink.
8. Incorporate the tomato paste along with the chopped rosemary, thyme, oregano, and paprika powder, stirring well to coat the meat.
9. Add the cubed potatoes and chopped bell pepper to the pot, letting them sauté for about 2 minutes.
10. Pour in the beef broth and canned diced tomatoes, season to your liking with salt and pepper, bring the mixture to a gentle bubble, and allow it to simmer uncovered for approximately 25 minutes.
11. Ladle the hot farmer's stew into individual serving bowls, topping each portion with a dollop of sour cream and a sprinkle of the reserved fresh herbs.
12. Serve immediately and savor every bite!
