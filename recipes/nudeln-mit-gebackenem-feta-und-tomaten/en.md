title: "Pasta with Baked Feta and Tomatoes"
---

## Preparation

1. Warm your oven up to 200°C.
2. Arrange the cherry tomatoes evenly across a baking tray. Coat them with a splash of oil along with a pinch of salt and pepper, tossing thoroughly to combine.
3. Position the feta block right in the middle of the tray, flipping it a couple of times so the oil coats all sides.
4. Roast in the oven for roughly 35 minutes, watching for the tomatoes to soften and split open, and the cheese to achieve a creamy, melted texture.
5. In the meantime, prepare your pasta following the package guidelines. Once done, strain well and reserve.
6. Take the tray out of the oven. Mince the garlic cloves finely and blend them directly into the roasted mixture.
7. Strip roughly half of the basil leaves into manageable pieces and fold them into the tray contents.
8. Add the cooked pasta to the baking sheet and toss all the ingredients together until thoroughly combined and coated.
9. Top with the remaining basil leaves for garnish.
10. Serve right away and enjoy!
