title: "Potato Spinach Casserole"
---

## Preparation

1. Slice the potatoes thinly and arrange them evenly inside a baking dish.
2. Mince the onion until fine.
3. In a saucepan, combine the butter, flour, milk, and vegetable broth.
4. Toss in the minced onions and fresh spinach, cooking the mixture just until the spinach wilts down.
5. Season the sauce with a pinch of nutmeg, salt, and pepper, then pour it uniformly over the layer of potatoes.
6. Dice the mozzarella and scatter it across the top of the dish, followed by a generous dusting of parmesan.
7. Place the casserole into an oven preheated to 180°C and bake for 30 minutes until golden and bubbling.
8. Serve hot and enjoy!
