## Preparation

1. Prepare the brown rice by following the package directions.
2. In a small bowl, dissolve the cornstarch in one-fifth of the total water. Mince the peeled garlic clove.
3. Pour the remaining water, soy sauce, honey, minced garlic, and ginger into a small saucepan, and bring the mixture to a rolling boil.
4. Whisk the cornstarch slurry into the boiling sauce, reduce the heat, and let it simmer for roughly 2 minutes, stirring constantly. Take the pan off the heat and let the sauce cool down to room temperature.
5. Dice the chicken breast into even, bite-sized pieces.
6. Separate one-fifth of the prepared sauce and set it aside for later. Toss the chicken pieces in the rest of the sauce, cover the container, and let them marinate in the fridge for approximately 15 minutes.
7. Remove the core from the bell pepper and cut the flesh into thin strips; break the broccoli down into individual florets.
8. Bring a small amount of water to a boil in a pot. Set a steamer basket or fine-mesh sieve containing the broccoli over the water, cover with a lid, and steam for about 15 minutes until tender.
9. Warm the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat, add the bell pepper strips, and cook for about 10 minutes, stirring from time to time. Remove the peppers and set them aside.
10. In the same skillet over medium heat, cook the marinated chicken pieces for roughly 4 minutes, turning them until they are completely cooked through.
11. Thinly slice the scallion into delicate rings.
12. Spoon the cooked rice into individual serving bowls, top with the steamed broccoli, sautéed bell pepper, and cooked chicken, and finish by drizzling the reserved sauce over each bowl, garnishing with the sliced scallions and sesame seeds.
