title: "Roasted Salmon Fillet with Oven Vegetables"
---

## Preparation

1. Begin by thoroughly rinsing the zucchini and spring onions. Cut them into slender rounds and transfer them into a mixing bowl.
2. Slice the cherry tomatoes in half and toss them in with the other vegetables.
3. Season the produce mixture with a tablespoon of lemon juice, a teaspoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, freshly ground pepper, paprika, chili flakes, and thyme, tossing until evenly coated. Spread the mixture in an even layer across a parchment-lined baking sheet.
4. Nestle the salmon fillets directly over the bed of seasoned vegetables.
5. Mince or press the garlic cloves, scattering them evenly across both the fish and the vegetables.
6. Splash the remaining lemon juice over the top, adding a final touch of salt and pepper to taste.
7. Transfer to a preheated oven set to 180°C (350°F) and bake for approximately 25 minutes, or until the salmon is fully cooked and the vegetables achieve a tender, lightly roasted finish.
8. Take the dish out of the oven and adorn it with fresh sprigs of dill and thyme before serving.
9. Serve hot and savor your dish!
