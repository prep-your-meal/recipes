## Preparation

1. Begin by removing the onion's outer layers and chopping it into small cubes. Core and de-seed the bell pepper, then slice it into manageable, bite-sized chunks.
2. Warm the olive oil in a suitable pot over a medium flame, tossing in the onions and peppers to cook down for approximately 3 minutes.
3. While the vegetables soften, peel the garlic and mince it directly into the pot using a garlic press, continuing to cook for an additional minute.
4. Introduce the ground turkey to the mixture, using a wooden utensil to crumble it into smaller segments as it browns for about 4 minutes. Sprinkle in the chili powder, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper.
5. Pour in the canned diced tomatoes, chicken stock, drained kidney beans, and sweet corn. Stir thoroughly to combine all the ingredients, then reduce the heat and allow the mixture to gently simmer for roughly 25 minutes, remembering to stir from time to time.
6. Ladle the hot chili into bowls, top with a dollop of sour cream, and enjoy immediately.
