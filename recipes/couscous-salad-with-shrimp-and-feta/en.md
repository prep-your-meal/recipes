title: "Couscous Salad with Shrimp and Feta"
---

## Preparation

1. Heat the vegetable stock until it reaches a rolling boil, then stir in the couscous. Take the pan off the heat source and allow the grains to absorb the liquid and plump up for roughly 5 minutes.
2. While the couscous is resting, dice the tomatoes and cucumber into bite-sized bits, cut the fresh mint into delicate ribbons, and thinly slice the garlic.
3. Prepare the dressing in a compact bowl by whisking together the lemon juice and half of the total olive oil, then season to taste with salt and pepper.
4. Warm the remaining olive oil in a skillet over medium heat, tossing in the garlic and pine nuts until they become fragrant and take on a golden hue.
5. Toss the shrimp into the skillet with the garlic and nuts, cooking them for approximately 3 minutes until just done.
6. Combine the couscous, chopped vegetables, mint, dressing, and cooked shrimp mixture in a generously sized bowl. Break the feta cheese into chunks with your hands directly over the salad. Give everything a thorough toss to integrate the flavors before plating.
7. Enjoy your meal!
