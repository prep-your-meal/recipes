title: "Mexican Ground Beef Casserole"
---

## Instructions

1. Begin by finely dicing the onion. Remove the seeds from the bell pepper and chop it into small, uniform pieces.
2. Warm a generous drizzle of olive oil in a spacious, deep skillet set over medium heat. Add the ground beef and cook for roughly 4 minutes, breaking it apart with a utensil until it is thoroughly browned and crumbly.
3. Drain the canned corn and incorporate it into the skillet alongside the prepared onion and bell pepper, continuing to sauté the mixture for 5 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, prepare the pasta following the package directions, and set your oven to preheat to 180 °C (350 °F).
5. Pour the diced tomatoes and their juices into the skillet, stirring well, and allow the sauce to simmer gently for 10 minutes.
6. Infuse the sauce with flavor by seasoning it with garlic powder, ground cumin, paprika, salt, pepper, chili flakes, and dried oregano, stirring to evenly distribute the spices.
7. Once the pasta is done, drain it thoroughly and transfer it to a baking dish, tossing it together with the rich meat sauce until fully combined.
8. Scatter the cheese evenly over the top of the dish and bake on the center oven rack for approximately 15 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and beautifully golden brown.
9. Chop the green onion into thin rings and scatter them over the casserole as a fresh garnish just before dishing it out.
10. Serve hot and savor your meal!
