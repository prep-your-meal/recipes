---

## Preparation

1. Start by skinning the potatoes, then transfer them to a pot of water and cook for 15 to 20 minutes until they can be easily pierced with a fork.
2. While the potatoes are on the stove, rinse the cucumbers thoroughly and cut them into thin rounds using either a sharp chef's knife or a mandoline.
3. Transfer the sliced cucumbers to a mixing bowl, combining them with the soy yogurt, chopped fresh dill, and a splash of lemon juice. Add salt and pepper to suit your palate.
4. Once the potatoes are tender, drain them, slice them into bite-sized pieces, and carefully incorporate them into the cucumber mixture.
5. Optional: For the best flavor development, chill the salad in the refrigerator for approximately one hour. Give it a final toss, taste for seasoning, and serve.
