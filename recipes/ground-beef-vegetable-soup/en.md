## Preparation

1. Begin by removing the onion's outer layers and chopping it into small, uniform cubes. Cut the celery stalks into neat slices.
2. Warm the olive oil in a soup pot over a medium setting, then add your prepared onion and celery, cooking them gently for roughly 3 minutes until they begin to soften.
3. Incorporate the ground beef into the pot, using a wooden spoon to crumble the meat. Sprinkle in the salt, black pepper, and garlic powder, continuing to cook and brown the mixture for approximately 10 minutes.
4. Pour in the vegetable broth and the diced tomatoes to deglaze the bottom of the pot, stirring well, and season with the dried basil. Allow the mixture to bubble at a medium simmer for about 20 minutes.
5. Drop the green beans into the simmering broth and let the soup cook for an additional 10 minutes.
6. While the soup simmers, finely mince the fresh parsley leaves.
7. Turn off the heat or lower it, then stir the shredded cheddar directly into the broth until it completely melts into a creamy consistency. Ladle into bowls, topping each serving with the minced parsley and an extra pinch of cheddar if you like.
