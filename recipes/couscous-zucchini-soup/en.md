---

## Preparation

1. Thoroughly rinse the tomatoes, zucchini, and onion, then chop all three into uniform, bite-sized cubes.
2. Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a saucepan, sweat the onion until translucent, toss in the zucchini and tomatoes, and sauté the mixture for roughly 3 minutes.
3. Pour the vegetable stock into the pot along with the rest of the ingredients, bringing the liquid to a gentle simmer over a medium flame for approximately 15 minutes.
4. Adjust the seasoning by adding salt, pepper, and paprika powder to suit your palate.
5. Ladle into bowls while piping hot and savor your meal!
