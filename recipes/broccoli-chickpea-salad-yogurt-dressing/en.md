title: "Broccoli Chickpea Salad with Yogurt Dressing"
---

## Preparation

1. Separate the broccoli into bite-sized pieces, submerge them in boiling water, and let them boil for 4 minutes.
2. Mince the garlic finely, then combine it in a compact bowl with the yogurt, lime juice, and dill.
3. Break apart the feta cheese manually into coarse crumbles and stir them into the sauce mixture. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.
4. Strain the liquid from both the broccoli and the chickpeas, then combine them together in a large mixing bowl.
5. Chop the red pepper into small cubes, toss them into the bowl with the broccoli and chickpeas, and coat the entire salad evenly with the prepared yogurt dressing before serving.
