## Preparation

1. Warm your oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Begin by removing the skins from the sweet potatoes, then chop the flesh into bite-sized cubes.
3. Toss the sweet potato pieces with a pinch of salt. Spread them evenly over a parchment-lined baking tray and roast on the center oven rack for approximately 15 minutes, or until tender.
4. While the potatoes are in the oven, rinse and drain the black beans, then toss them gently with the ground cumin.
5. Prepare the avocado by peeling away the skin, discarding the pit, and slicing the flesh into neat cubes.
6. Mince the onion and garlic clove finely.
7. Roughly chop the tomatoes, then transfer them to a food processor along with the lemon juice, minced onion, and garlic. Pulse until smooth to create a fresh salsa.
8. Mince the fresh cilantro.
9. To assemble, divide the seasoned black beans, roasted sweet potatoes, and avocado cubes among serving bowls. Spoon the homemade tomato salsa generously over the top and garnish with the freshly chopped cilantro.
10. Serve immediately and enjoy your meal!
