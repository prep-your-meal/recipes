---

## Preparation

1. In a saucepan, bring the vegetable broth and red curry paste to a boil, then incorporate the couscous and take the pan off the burner.
2. Allow the couscous to absorb the liquid while you finely mince the bell peppers, spring onions, and fresh cilantro, then gently fold these vegetables into the couscous.
3. Whisk the vinegar, olive oil, soy sauce, and tomato paste together in a compact dish to form a dressing, and thoroughly stir it into the couscous mixture.
4. Complete the dish by seasoning with salt, pepper, ground cumin, and chili flakes according to your preference.
5. Serve and enjoy!
