## Preparation

1. Submerge the potatoes in a pot of water and bring to a simmer for about 20 minutes until tender.
2. While the potatoes cook, remove the onion skin and chop it into fine dice. Sweat the diced onion in a saucepan using a splash of rapeseed oil.
3. Pour in the vegetable broth to deglaze the pan, then whisk in the white wine vinegar, mustard, salt, and black pepper to form a warm dressing.
4. Drain the cooked potatoes, allow them to cool down just enough to handle, and gently peel away the skins.
5. Cut the peeled potatoes into uniform slices, combine them thoroughly with the prepared dressing, and set aside to marinate for a minimum of 30 minutes.
6. Mince the chives finely, fold them gently into the potato mixture, and plate for serving.
7. Enjoy!
