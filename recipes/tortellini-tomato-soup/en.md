title: "Tortellini Tomato Soup"
---

## Preparation

1. Combine the vegetable broth and diced tomatoes in a saucepan, bringing the mixture to a quick boil.
2. Using an immersion blender, blend the soup until completely smooth, then gently fold in the tortellini and torn fresh basil.
3. Cook at a gentle simmer for an additional 5 minutes until the pasta is tender.
4. Ladle into bowls while piping hot and serve immediately.
