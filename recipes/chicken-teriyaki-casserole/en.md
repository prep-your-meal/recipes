## Preparation

1. Warm your oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Measure out 1 tablespoon of water and keep it on hand. Combine the honey, soy sauce, and the rest of the water in a small pot with a lid, then bring the mixture to a boil over the heat.
3. Take the lid off as soon as it boils, and allow the liquid to gently bubble for another minute.
4. While that heats, whisk the cornstarch into your reserved tablespoon of water until smooth, then pour it into the simmering sauce to help it thicken. Let it cook for roughly one more minute.
5. Arrange the chicken breast fillets inside a baking dish (roughly 21 x 15 cm) and coat them thoroughly with about half of the prepared sauce. Place the dish on the center oven rack and bake for approximately 30 minutes.
6. While the chicken cooks, prepare your mixed vegetables and brown rice following the directions on their packages. At the same time, set aside 1 tablespoon of the remaining sauce in a separate small bowl.
7. Once the baking time is up, pull the chicken out and use two forks to pull the fillets apart into fine shreds.
8. Combine the shredded chicken directly in the baking dish with the prepared rice, vegetables, and the rest of the sauce, stirring well. Return the dish to the middle rack of the oven for another 15 minutes of baking.
9. Drizzle your reserved tablespoon of sauce over the top of the finished casserole right before serving.
