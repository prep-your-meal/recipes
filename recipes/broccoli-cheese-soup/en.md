## Preparation

1. Separate the broccoli head into individual florets, then drop them into a pot of boiling water and cook for 5 minutes.
2. While the broccoli is simmering, take the shallots and chop them roughly.
3. Strain the cooked broccoli thoroughly. Transfer it to a container along with the milk, grated Parmesan, chopped shallots, and vegetable stock, and process using an immersion blender until the mixture achieves a silky, uniform consistency.
4. Pour the blended soup back onto the heat, bring it to a gentle boil for 4 minutes, and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper according to your preference.
5. Ladle into bowls and serve immediately!
